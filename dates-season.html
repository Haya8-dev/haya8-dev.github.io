Unaizah International Dates Season

A web platform developed for Unaizah International Dates Season, designed to provide visitors with event information and support digital booking and auction-related experiences.

Role Web Developer
Type Event Web Platform
Year 2026

Project Overview

The platform was developed to support the digital presence of the season and provide visitors with an organized way to explore events, auctions and booking experiences. The project combines public-facing pages with backend functionality and database-driven features.

Key Features

01

Event Information

Dedicated sections for presenting season activities, experiences and important visitor information.

02

Auction Booking

A booking experience that allows visitors to reserve seats for selected auction events.

03

Seat Management

Backend logic for tracking booking capacity and managing available seats.

04

Booking Confirmation

Reservation data is processed and stored, with confirmation functionality for completed bookings.

05

Database Integration

MySQL integration is used to manage reservation records and application data.

06

Administrative Tools

Backend administrative functionality supports the management and review of booking information.

My Role

My work focused on the development and technical implementation of the platform, including backend functionality, database integration and booking-related features.

  • Developed and structured website functionality.
  • Built PHP-based backend features.
  • Connected the platform to a MySQL database.
  • Developed booking and reservation logic.
  • Implemented seat and capacity management.
  • Worked on booking confirmation and email functionality.
  • Tested and prepared the system for deployment.
